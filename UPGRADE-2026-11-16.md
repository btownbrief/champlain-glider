Branch `upgrade-2026-11-16`. It follows the ranked plan in `ADVISOR.md`. Stephen's brief was: playable in portrait, not too hard, not repetitive, a reason to come back.

## What changed, in the advisor's order

### 1. Portrait is the main way to play (done)

- The rotate overlay, its CSS and its media rule are deleted.
- `TUNE.viewPortrait` sets the portrait camera:
  - base scale 0.44
  - anchorX 0.20, birdLow 0.50
  - zoomFloor 0.5, birdHigh 0.22, groundFracFar 1.0. These three differ from the advisor's 0.62 / 0.34 / (none); see below.
- The bird is drawn at `max(worldScale, 0.72)`, so zooming out never makes it a dot. Collisions stay in world units. It has a soft cream halo, so it reads on every ground color.
- **Fixed scale per orientation:** the world scale (`baseScale`) is set once per orientation or real width change. Safari's toolbar changing the height no longer rescales the world (tested).
- The ticker hides when a run starts and shows again at game over.
- **Why the camera numbers differ:**
  - With birdHigh 0.34, a big flight in portrait left the landing site below the screen.
  - The bird is now always drawn at least 50 px, so the camera can pull further out. birdHigh 0.22 and a ground limit at the screen bottom keep the landing in view. The ground is off-screen 9.5% of portrait flying time.
  - Landscape got the same idea: zoomFloor 0.22 (was 0.42) and groundFracFar 1.02 (was 1.15). Ground off-screen dropped from 20% to 13%.

### 2. The night is a lead you can spend (done, tuned)

- **Pace by island, not by your speed:** the night's speed comes from the island you are on, `900 + 190 * (island - 1)`. From island 6 it also adds `15 * (island - 6)^2`.
  - The advisor proposed 850 + 170 with no curve. That left the near-perfect bot immortal, because `damping 0.7` lets anyone faster than 0.7x the night stay ahead forever. The small late curve is what ends perfect runs at 3 to 5 minutes.
- **Easing:** it eases into each island's pace over about 10 s (`ease 0.3`) rather than 3 s. With 3 s, the bots' deaths piled up right at each island's shore.
- `maxGap 2600`, `startGap 2000`, `pushback 300` (Fever 450). `damping` and `airSafety` are unchanged.
- **Visible threat:** a dark edge with a breathing violet front grows in from the left once the gap is under 1,500 px. The sun dial is scaled to the new 2,600 range.
- **New island:** the dial pulses, a tick plays, and the banner says "Night quickens".
- `matchStart`, `matchEnd`, `matchRamp` and the 2,800 night cap are removed.

### 3. Water is a skill moment (done, rules adjusted after testing)

- **Softer splash:** `waterDrag 2.2 -> 1.2` and `waterMinSpeed 620 -> 1000`.
- **Skips:**
  - Finger up at contact gives a skip: a rising "plip", spray, "Skip x2", and +100 x the chain.
  - Holding gives a splash.
  - **Changed from the plan:** the skip angle limit is 45 degrees (`vy/vx < 1.0`), not 0.55. Testing showed nobody ever reached the water at a shallow angle. Gliders sail clear over the strait, and divers hit it steep. The simple rule "let go before you hit the water" now always works.
- **Water geometry, also changed:**
  - The strait is 3 keys wide (was 2).
  - The ground under each ramp lip is now a sheer cliff (0.28 spacing, was 0.6). Before, divers landed on a long cliff face and slid into the water. That was the advisor's "belly flop".
- **No crawl after a splash:** the beach out of every strait is a straight carry-up ramp. Bots showed a splash followed by a 3 to 4 s crawl up the shore at 560 px/s, which was still a run-ender.
- A one-time coach line, "LET GO OVER THE WATER / to skip like a stone", shows before the first crossing.

### 4. Short stages, one twist each, never the same twice in a row (done)

- **Island lengths:** island 1 is 22 keys (Sugarbush, first ramp at about 300 m). Islands 2 to 4 are 28 to 36 keys. Later islands keep their old lengths.
- **Today's tour:**
  - The biome order comes from a hashed day seed. Without the hash, consecutive dates opened with the same island.
  - Islands 1 to 4 are always four different biomes. After that, a biome never repeats back to back.
  - Weights were rebalanced so twist islands come up more often. The Long Trail had been island 2 on 40 of 60 days.
- **Stowe in October:** a leaf-swirl thermal every 6 to 8 keys. Soaring through one (finger up) lifts you, scatters leaves, and leads to a hoop. The old invisible global lift is removed.
- **Quechee Gorge:**
  - 2 or 3 real gaps per island, with a river at the bottom. The river uses the same skip and splash rules.
  - Clearing a gap without touching the river pops "Gorge jump +300".
  - The far wall is a carry-out ramp.
  - The gorge's base hills were reduced (ampMul 1.55 -> 1.15), and the invisible gorge headwind is removed. The gaps are the challenge now.
- **Champlain on Ice:** zero ground friction, plus a long ice-spray trail.
- **Long Trail Down:** twice the coin trails.
- **Added for fairness:**
  - Big-hill biomes (Green Mountains, Kingdom, Gorge) grow to full height over the first six islands. An early Green Mountains had been a wall that killed every tapper run of that day at the same spot.
  - The landing pits after jump hills are shallower.
- **Banner:** island names and town signs show in a fixed banner slot, top center.

### 5. Today's tour, fresh hills every run (done)

- **Split seeds:** the itinerary comes from the date. Hill shapes, coins and decorations come from `daySeed * 1000 + runsToday`. Tested: three runs in a row produce different hills on the same tour.
- **New key `champlain_glider_day`:** `{ day, runs, best, streak, lastDay }`.
- **Title:** shows "Today's tour: Sugarbush -> Long Trail -> Green Mtns -> Ice".
- **Streak:** a "Day N in a row" badge appears from day 2. Skipping a day resets it to 1 with no nagging. Tested by moving the clock forward one day and then three days.
- **Game over:** shows "Today X · Best Y".
- `champlain_glider_best` is untouched.

### 6. Three goals at a time (done)

- **Slots:** a new key, `champlain_glider_goalslots`, stores `{ slots, done }`. `champlain_glider_goals` is still the completed count, so the multiplier and the thrush unlock work as before.
- **Migration:** old saves migrate. The first N of the original ten goals are marked done.
- **Dealing:**
  - One easy goal is always dealt.
  - New goals: "Skip off the water", "Skip off the water 3 times", "Ride 2 leaf thermals", "Clear a gorge gap", "Reach island 4".
  - "Skip 3 times in one crossing" became "3 times" overall, because one crossing gives 1 or 2 skips.
  - Thermal and gorge goals are dealt only on days whose tour reaches that biome within six islands.
- **In the run:**
  - Three chips show for 3 s at the start.
  - A quiet "Coins 6/12" tick appears when a goal moves (quarter steps, throttled).
  - Completing one gives a gold chip, a chime, and "Multiplier xN".
  - Each slot completes at most once per run. The new goal starts counting next run, so a strong player can't chain six goals in 40 s.
- **Test results:**
  - A fresh save shows Grab 12 coins / Skip off the water / Chain 4.
  - The tapper finished 4 goals in its first run.

### 7. Subtraction (done)

- **Title:** the logo, "Hold to dive. Let go to soar.", bird chips, today's tour, and one big button. The streak badge appears only from day 2. On a portrait title the bird and nest sit below the logo.
- **Mute:** a 40 px round icon button under BEST.
- **Popups:**
  - All popups share one lane above the bird: at most 2, clamped 12 px inside the screen edges.
  - When the coach is up, the lane sits above it. When the bird is too near the top, the coach and lane flip under the bird. They never enter the score row or the banner and chip slot.
  - Changed from the plan: the advisor said to hide popups while the coach is up. The crest coach is up so often that "Perfect!" would rarely be seen, so the lane moves above the coach instead.
- **Combo line:** the chain sits under the score and becomes "FEVER x2". The separate fever banner is gone.
- **Other birds:**
  - Rivals are labelled by species ("Passed a blue jay! +800").
  - Removed: usernames, "joined the run", chat, "takes the lead", "dropped out".
  - Rivals are drawn smaller and translucent, so the player is always the brightest bird.
  - The "fellow traveler" birds are removed entirely. They added another bird to the field, and one of their lines named a real person.
- **Text removed:**
  - The per-coin "+25" popups. The coin sound and the bird's chomp already say it.
  - The bottom "The night is closing in" text. The night's edge and the dial now carry it.
- **Game over:** shows "Board score N" (no "(progress normalized)"). The retry button is visible without scrolling, under the postcard. The postcard stamp no longer covers its text. The run HUD (score, boost, dial) steps aside on the title and game over.
- **Ground patterns:** the polka-dot ground pattern is at half contrast.

### 8. One tap to flying (done)

- The wake-up is 0.65 s, and the first press counts as the first hold. Measured with a real CDP touch from page load: under control in 815 to 962 ms.

## Known issues and risks

- **Clustered deaths on one day:** a consistent player still dies within the same 300 to 500 m band on a given day. That is the island where the night outpaces them. Across days the spot moves, because the tour changes. The advisor's strict "no more than a third of deaths in any 100 m window" holds over several days but not always in a 3-run sample from a single day.
- **The holder does too well:** the never-let-go holder now survives about 70 to 130 s, sometimes longer than the tapper. Holding through every landing still scores perfect slides. That is physics the advisor asked to keep.
- **Skips are rare for strong players:** they fly clear over the water. Skips mostly rescue divers who let go late.
- **Higher displayed scores:** the displayed score climbs faster than before, because up to three goals can complete per run. The leaderboard uses the board score, which is unaffected.
- **Not on a real iPhone:** everything was tested headless in Chromium with phone emulation, not on a real device. Real Safari toolbar and notch behavior still need a look on Stephen's phone.
- **No fresh-reviewer pass:** no subagent tool was available in this session.
- **Towns repeat:** the 15 towns still repeat after 18,000 m.

### Done

1. **The night is a chase you can see and survive.**
   - `maxGap` 2,600 -> 4,200 and island pace about 9% faster (`baseSpeed` 980, `islandStep` 205, `lateRamp` 22).
   - **Changed from the plan:** raising the cap alone left the lead pinned at 4,200 for most of a run and then gone in one island (night close 6% of the time, measured). So there is a new **leash**: past 1,400 px the night closes a little faster the bigger your lead (`leash 0.3`). A good stretch still banks a lead, but it drifts back toward where you can see the night.
   - The night now slows harder when it is close (`damping 0.62` inside 1,400 px, was 0.7 inside 1,000). The almost-caught moment lasts long enough to claw back.
   - **After a slam on land the ground speed is floored at 1,000 px/s for 1.6 s** (`slamFloor`), the same fix the water already had. No more 560 px/s crawl.
   - The dark edge was already 16% of the width at 700 px, so it was kept as is.
2. **Holding forever no longer pays.**
   - A slide counts as Perfect only if the dive began **in the air on that flight**. The game watches for a fresh press after takeoff, so this works for touch, Space and the test bots alike.
   - Holding up a hill for more than 0.3 s bleeds speed (`uphillBleed 3.0`/s). This is skipped on ramps, water and a player's very first tutorial flight.
   - Result: the holder went from the longest-lived style to the shortest. The others moved by only a few seconds.
3. **The day shuffles, and it always has a twist.**
   - Each day picks four different islands (`DAY_SET`). Island 1 is always Sugarbush, and Stowe or the Gorge is always among the four.
   - Every run flies islands 2-4 in a fresh order, then a fresh draw from island 5 on, never the same biome back to back. Hills were already fresh every run.
   - The title says "Today's islands: Sugarbush · Gorge · Green Mtns · Ice".
   - Goals that need Stowe or the Gorge are dealt only on days that include them.
4. **Both flat twists now do something.**
   - **Ice:** 8-11 keys wherever it falls (about 11 s), with a snowdrift kicker every 1-2 keys. Each kicker has coins up its face, a hoop off the lip and a long back slope to dive onto.
   - **Thermals:** a pale band of warm air plus rising cream chevrons with a soft dark under-stroke, readable in a still frame from 600-700 px away (`s-thermal.png`).
5. **Text cut to one line.**
   - At most one popup at a time, and none while a coach cue is up. Routine ones (Perfect, Slam, Splash, Thermal, Boing) only speak when the line has been quiet for 3-4 s.
   - "Perfect!" shows when a chain starts and every 5th slide; the counter under the score carries the rest (shown from 2).
   - "FEVER!" no longer stacks with "Perfect! x3". Removed "Fever over", "Clean launch", "Cloud", "BOOST!" and "Hoop!" text, keeping their points; they speak with sound, particles and a small shake.
   - One rival per island, never your own species, and the pass bonus pays once per rival.
   - The crest coach ("KEEP HOLDING" / "LET GO!") retires after your first two flights (new key `champlain_glider_crestruns`).
6. **Something to earn tomorrow.**
   - At most one goal completes per flight ("Goals · one per flight" on the game over). The multiplier and the Thrush now take days.
   - **Daily stamp:** reach island 5 on today's tour to stamp the day: a banner and chime in flight, a check on the title. The streak badge now counts stamped days in a row; missing a day just starts it over, with no nagging. Stored in the existing `champlain_glider_day` record (new `stamped` field).
   - **Game over:** one headline number (points). Under it sit the stamp state and your best. The board number moved into the leaderboard block as "Monthly board (no multiplier): this flight N".
   - The title's credit links moved to one line on the bottom edge, above the news ticker, about 46 px below the start button in landscape.
   - Hoops, coins and thermals are no longer drawn behind the game-over text.
7. **Found while testing like a player:** a thumb that started on "Hold to fly again" and drifted lost its hold, because the game-over panel scrolls and the browser cancelled the touch. The live site does this too. Fixed with `touch-action: none` on the play buttons.

### Skipped or partly met, and why

- **Ice airborne share is 54%, target 55%.** Close enough to leave; the number includes the exit ramp and the water crossing.
- **Ice over 15 s:** 2 of 33 ice islands ran 16-18 s. Both were slow crossings after a splash, which count toward the island.
- **Full lead to caught for the casual player is 5.2 s, target 6.** Tapper and lag are well above. Raising it further made everyone live much longer, which works against "fair but real difficulty".
- **Night within 1,500 px for the skilled lag bot is 9%, below the 20-35% band.** The tapper (30%) and holder (31%) are inside it, and the casual player is close (15%). A skilled player keeps the night farther away, which seems right.
- **The displayed score still grows with the goal multiplier**, so "New best!" comes easily in the first days. The board score that the leaderboard uses is unaffected. The multiplier now grows by at most 1 a flight.
